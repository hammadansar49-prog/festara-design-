Questions

Asked before every event.

Do guests need to download an app?

No. The invite opens in the phone's browser, the same way a WhatsApp link does.

What kinds of events can I plan?

Weddings and their functions, trips with friends, university fests, society meetups, Eid milans, birthdays. Anything with a date, a place and people.

Who can change the date or place?

Only Admins can edit an event. Members help plan, and Guests only see the date, time and place.

Can one big event have separate parts?

Yes. Make each part its own event, like Mehndi and Walima, or day one and day two of a fest, each with its own link and people.

How do I invite people?

Share one link on WhatsApp or anywhere else. You choose whether the link makes people Members or Guests.

What happens when the invite link is old?

Links expire after seven days so they can't float around forever. An Admin can make a new one in a tap.
