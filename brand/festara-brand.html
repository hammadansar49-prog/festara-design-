Essence

What Festara is, and who it is for

Festara is for groups of 3 to 50 people who organize one event together and have no shared place to do it. It is not a ticketing site, a vendor directory or a payment app.

The name. Fest, from festival, plus tara, the Urdu word for star. The star is the mark: an eight-pointed shape that can also be read as a marigold flower seen from above.

The position. Splitwise settles money after the fact. WhatsApp holds the conversation and loses the decisions. Wedding software charges planners. Festara is the free, shared page that sits between them, for the people actually doing the work.

Example people used throughout this book. They stand in for the three situations in the project proposal.

Four principles

Calm app, colorful events

Screens stay quiet and trustworthy. Color arrives with the event the group is planning, so the personality is theirs, not ours.

Money is a first-class citizen

Budget figures are large, aligned and never decorative. A warning says how much is left, not just that something is wrong.

Desi in the material, not the costume

Roots show in the palette, the star, the words (Mehndi, Walima, Bride's side) and rupee formatting. No paisley clip art, no truck-art borders.

Every element earns its place

If removing a shadow, icon tile or label loses nothing, it goes. A choice made by reflex is the thing we are avoiding.

Voice

Plain, warm and specific

Write the way a capable cousin would explain it: short sentences, real numbers, names of real things. Say what happened and what to do next.

Always

  • Name the event, the person and the amount.
  • Buttons say the action: Create event, Copy invite link, Delete event.
  • Use the family's words: Mehndi, Walima, Bride's side, Groom's side.
  • Write money as Rs 412,000, with the Rs first and a space.

Never

  • Emoji as decoration, sparkles, or stacked exclamation marks.
  • Seamless, effortless, supercharge, unlock, journey, magic.
  • Error codes or system words (token, payload, 403) shown to people.
  • "Oops" or an apology where a next step belongs.
Logo

A lowercase wordmark and a star

The wordmark is set in Fraunces with its soft axis on, weight 560. The star is two squares, one turned 45 degrees, with a marigold core. The star alone is the favicon and app icon.

How the star is built

Inner corners sit at 0.765 of the outer radius. The core is a quarter of the star's radius.

Star on its own

Marigold star on an ink tile for icons. Ink star with a marigold core on paper. Never marigold alone on paper, it reads at only 2 to 1.

Clearspace

Keep one star height clear on every side. Nothing enters this space, including other logos.

Minimum size

Wordmark 20 px high. Star on its own 12 px. Favicon 16 px, where only the marigold shape is read.

Color

Ink on paper, paper on ink. The marigold core stays marigold on both. Never recolor to an event color.

Do not

Stretch it or tint it with an event color.
Add shadows, gradients or outlines.
Place it on marigold or any busy ground.

Money

Write Rs 412,000. In code: `Rs ${new Intl.NumberFormat('en-PK').format(n)}`. Pakistani readers also know lakh grouping (4,12,000). If the team prefers it, use en-IN in the same line.

Digits in Hanken Grotesk are already tabular (every digit is 560 units wide, checked in the font file), so columns of amounts align without a font feature.

Rules

  • Body copy 16 px. UI can drop to 14. Never below 12.
  • Inputs stay at 16 px on phones so iOS does not zoom.
  • Headings use balanced wrapping. Paragraphs cap near 62 characters.
  • No Fraunces below 24 px, no weight under 400.
Shape and space

Borders over shadows, radii by role

A four-pixel grid. Corner radius says what an object is: small for controls, larger for cards, round only for people and status. One shadow exists, for things that float.

Radius

6 px, buttons, inputs, selects
12 px, cards, sheets, dialogs
Full, avatars, chips

Spacing

4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64. Related items sit closer than unrelated ones, so most grouping needs no box at all.

One shadow

Popovers, sheets and dialogs float and get the overlay shadow. Cards sit flat with a 1 px line. In dark mode the shadow becomes a hairline plus depth.

Motion

Fast on touch, soft on arrival

Motion confirms what the user did or explains where something went. Controls respond on pointer-down. Things that arrive settle without bounce. Bounce is kept for objects the user threw.

Feel it

SpentRs 412,000

Buttons shrink to 97% in 100 ms on press. The meter eases in once and is static on later visits. Both respect reduced motion.

UseTiming
Press feedback100 ms, on pointer-down
Menus, tabs, toasts220 ms, ease out
Sheets, dialogs320 ms, spring, damping 1.0
Flicked or draggedSpring, damping 0.8, keeps release velocity
Reduced motionCross-fade only, no slide, no scale

Animate

  • A sheet or menu leaving the way it came in.
  • A status change, such as a budget turning amber.
  • A toast confirming a copy or a save.

Never animate

  • Numbers counting up. Money should be readable the moment it appears.
  • Moving backgrounds, floating blobs or glows.
  • Anything that blocks input while it plays.

Landing page

Where Aceternity is allowed

The public page can be a little more theatrical than the app. It still shows the product and uses the same tokens. Aceternity UI is limited to the landing page, and to two effects that each explain something.

Allowed, max two

  • The event card stack: three covers (mehndi, trip, fest) showing that one product holds all three kinds of event.
  • The budget meter filling to 82% and turning amber, to show the one thing competitors lack.
  • Real screenshots of the built app once screens exist, placed in plain frames.

Banned

  • Purple-to-blue gradients, glow orbs, aurora and beam backgrounds.
  • Three equal feature cards with icon tiles, and glass cards stacked in glass cards.
  • Invented customer logos, testimonials, ratings or user counts.
  • Any effect that runs forever, or hides content until it animates in.

Section order follows the group's own journey: the problem in WhatsApp and spreadsheets, one shared page, the three event types, the budget warning, then a plain sign-up. The tone is the Voice section above.
